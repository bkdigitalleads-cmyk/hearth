Hearth — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Hearth ("the app") is a homeschool attendance and hours tracker for iPhone, made by Brian Kershner. It is designed so that your family's records stay on your device.

What we collect: almost nothing

Your homeschool log — student names, lesson entries, dates, hours, subjects, notes, work-sample photos, your state selection, and any names you enter — is stored only in the app's local database and private storage on your iPhone. It is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to us or anyone else. The app works fully offline and has no account system. Hearth does not use GPS and does not track anyone's location.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo-library access are used only to attach a photo of a work sample to a lesson entry. Images are resized and stored on-device only.

Purchases

If you buy Hearth Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to validate purchases. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous identifier and your purchase history for this app — never your name, your students' names, or your records. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Analytics and tracking

Hearth contains no analytics SDKs, no ads, and no tracking. We do not collect usage data.

Exports

PDF and CSV exports are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them (print, email, AirDrop, etc.).

Face ID

If you enable the app lock, authentication is handled entirely by iOS. Hearth never sees your biometric data.

Children

Hearth is intended for parents and guardians who homeschool. Because no personal data leaves the device, no personal data about anyone — including students — is collected by us.

State requirements

The state information shown in the app is a convenience summary compiled from public state sources and is not legal advice. Requirements change; confirm them with your state before relying on them.

Deleting your data

Delete any entry or student in the app, use Settings → Delete all records, or simply delete the app — everything it stored goes with it.
