Frequently asked questions

How do I keep homeschool attendance records?
Log each lesson with its date, hours, and subject. Any day with an entry counts as a school day. Hearth totals days and hours for the school year and exports an attendance log PDF with a signature line.

What if my state has no hours requirement?
Set the target your evaluator, umbrella school, or district expects, or use the default of 180 days. Everything else works the same.

Can I track more than one student?
Yes. Add each student in Settings and switch between them with one tap. Reports export per student or for everyone.

Is it free?
Your first 14 entries are free. Hearth Pro unlocks unlimited entries, the attendance and portfolio PDFs, CSV export, and Face ID lock, as a one-time Lifetime purchase or a Yearly subscription.
