What does Utah require homeschoolers to keep?

Days per year

Attendance log: Not required by the state

Days and hours

Districts may not require set hours or days (Utah Code 53G-6-204).

What to file and keep

Give a one-time notification to the local school board; the district may not require attendance records, records of instruction, curriculum, hours, or testing.

A simple record that satisfies most requests

Whatever Utah asks for, a daily log covers it: the date, which student, minutes or hours of instruction, the subjects touched, and a line of notes. Add a photo of one piece of work a week and you also have a portfolio. Keep the log all year and the end-of-year form is a matter of copying totals.

Frequently asked questions

How many days or hours of homeschool does Utah require?
Districts may not require set hours or days (Utah Code 53G-6-204).

Do I have to keep attendance records to homeschool in Utah?
Utah does not require an attendance log by statute. Most families keep one regardless: it is the fastest way to answer a district question, it feeds the year-end paperwork, and it settles any later dispute about days of instruction. A log with the date, hours and subjects for each school day is enough.

What do I file to homeschool in Utah?
Give a one-time notification to the local school board; the district may not require attendance records, records of instruction, curriculum, hours, or testing.
